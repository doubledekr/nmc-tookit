Your save file & the home office

Desktop app
Chrome extension (test) — one-click send from Salesforce
  1. In Chrome open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder below.
  3. Open any Salesforce lead — a red Send to NMC toolkit button appears bottom-right. Click it; the lead lands at the top of your Pipeline.

Updates: the folder is replaced with each toolkit update — restart Chrome to pick it up. Nothing leaves this computer; the extension talks to the app on 127.0.0.1 only.

Local save file

Everything you do saves on this computer, instantly, with or without a connection. Pick a folder and it also writes nmc-toolkit-you.json and call-blocks-you.csv there (Chrome/Edge) — a local folder or the mapped NMC drive.

No save folder yet — data still saves inside this browser automatically.
Home office hub
Pulling clients from Salesforce

With the hub: ask IT to add the \u201cSend to NMC toolkit\u201d button to the Salesforce lead page. Click it on any open lead and that client shows up at the top of your Pipeline as New from Salesforce the moment you switch back here.

Without the hub: drag this to your bookmarks bar (re-drag it after each toolkit update), click it on an open lead, then use Paste from clipboard & parse on the Client workspace. It reads every field on the lead — including sections Salesforce hides from a plain copy: Copy lead for NMC

Linking the hub turns on live rates, team stats, the template library, and a server backup of this save file. No VPN or offline? Everything core keeps working from your local copy and catches up on its own.

Salesforce bookmark

The “Copy lead for NMC” bookmark was updated

Browsers don’t let an app change your bookmarks for you, so this takes one drag. Your old bookmark keeps working until you replace it, but it won’t pick up the fixes.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it’s hidden (Ctrl+Shift+B, Mac ⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: Copy lead for NMC
  3. Delete the old Copy lead for NMC bookmark (right-click it → Delete) so there’s only one.

Can’t drag? then right-click the old bookmark → Edit and paste it over the URL.
